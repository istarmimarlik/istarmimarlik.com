Approach

A threshold is where two spaces meet. The relationship drawn between inside and outside, between shared and personal space, shapes how we use a place.

Our design focuses on these relationships. We think about layout, passages, light and material together, as part of everyday life.

  1. Geometry

    We explore how the needs come together in space, working on proportions, layout and voids together.

  2. Circulation

    We design entrances and passages not only to connect spaces but as places that set the flow of use.

  3. Material

    We weigh materials by their texture, their relationship with light, the conditions of use and how they change over time.

  4. Human experience

    We rethink every decision in the drawing at the scale of the person who will use the space.

Services

From architectural design to permit projects, from interiors to visualisation, we can work together on different needs.

Architectural Design

Starting from the conditions of the site and the brief, we develop designs for new buildings. We treat layout, plans, massing and facade as one whole.

Concept design · Planning · Massing and facade design · Design development · Project drawings

Interior Design

We design interiors through the needs of their use and the relationships between spaces. Planning, material, colour and lighting decisions are developed together.

Space planning · Material and colour choices · Lighting approach · Detail design

Permit Projects and Urban Regeneration

We prepare and develop architectural projects for new buildings and urban regeneration. The scope of work is set together, according to the project’s needs and the stage it is at.

Site feasibility · Schematic sections · Plan, section and elevation drawings

Architectural Visualisation and Animation

We tell building and interior projects through images and animation, producing visual content to evaluate design decisions, compare material options and present the project.

Exterior images · Interior images · Architectural animation

How do we work together?

  1. We listen to your project

    We begin by talking about your site, your space or the project you are working on, and look at the needs, priorities and the information at hand together.

  2. We set the scope

    We agree which stages we will work on together, the drawings and images to be prepared, the schedule and the fee, according to the project.

  3. We develop the project

    We work within the agreed scope and move design and project decisions forward through shared reviews.
